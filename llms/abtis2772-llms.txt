# AbTis

> AbTis Co., Ltd. is a South Korean biotechnology company (founded 2016, Yongin-si, Gyeonggi-do; an
> ADC-specialized subsidiary of Dong-A ST since its 2023 acquisition) that develops AbClick, a
> third-generation site-selective antibody conjugation platform. AbClick attaches payloads to the Fc
> Lys248 residue of native IgG antibodies with no enzyme and no Fc engineering, giving controlled
> drug-to-antibody ratio and batch-to-batch consistency, and is applied to ADC, DAC, AOC, ARC and
> ISAC modalities and dual-payload designs. AbTis licenses the platform, runs collaborative research
> and fee-for-service ADC development, and advances its own pipeline led by AT-211 (Claudin
> 18.2-targeting MMAE ADC). Its product is chemistry and intellectual property, not software.
